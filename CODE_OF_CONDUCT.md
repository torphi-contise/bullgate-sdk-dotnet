# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Bullgate community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression,
experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race,
caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive,
and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respect for differing views, constructive
feedback, responsibility for mistakes, and focus on community benefit.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or imagery, harassment,
trolling, insults, personal or political attacks, and publishing another
person's private information without permission.

## Enforcement and scope

Community leaders may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues,
and contributions that violate these standards. This Code applies in all
project spaces and when a person officially represents the project.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to `marco@torphi.com.br` with the
subject `Code of Conduct - Bullgate`. Do not publish sensitive reports in an
issue.

Community leaders may apply a private correction, formal warning, temporary
ban, or permanent ban according to severity, duration, and pattern.
